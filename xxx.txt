Good catch, and it was worse than the naming. isPastCustomerDate re-implemented
the ISO date regex and the calendar round-trip that src/i18n/dateFormats.ts
already had, byte for byte. That is now isPastIsoDate there, behind one shared
parseIsoLocalDate that formatIsoDmyDate uses as well. formatCustomerDate is
gone entirely — it was a one-line null guard over formatIsoDmyDate.

The two that remain are no longer exported and no longer claim to be about
customers: formatPostalAddress and formatYesNo, both private to the module,
since useCustomerFormatters is their only caller.

I kept the hook itself here. Its expiry member hardcodes
customers.details.compliance.status.overdue and the CDD/FATCA rule of stacking
the Overdue badge above the date, so that one is genuinely domain-bound.

On CustomerAddress: the shape is generic, but it types the addresses object of
the customer payload and the adapter that normalises it, so I left the name
matching the endpoint it models.
